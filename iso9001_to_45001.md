# ISO 45001 and ISO 9001: Relationship and Extensions

## Overview

ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety) and ISO 9001 (Quality Management) are international management system standards published by ISO. Both follow the same **High Level Structure (HLS) / Annex SL** framework, making them highly compatible and easy to integrate into a single management system.

---

## How They Relate

### Common Foundation

Both standards share:

- Identical clause numbering and titles (Context, Leadership, Planning, Support, Operation, Evaluation, Improvement)
- Core definitions and common text
- Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) methodology

### Key Differences

|Aspect|ISO 9001|ISO 45001|
|---|---|---|
|**Focus**|Quality of products/services|Occupational health & safety|
|**Goal**|Customer satisfaction|Worker safety & well-being|
|**Key concept**|Nonconformity of products|Hazards, risks, incidents|
|**Interested parties**|Customers, stakeholders|Workers, contractors, visitors|
|**Risk focus**|Quality risks|OH&S risks and opportunities|

---

## How ISO 45001 Extends ISO 9001

### Clause 4 – Context of the Organization

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Identify internal/external issues affecting quality|+ Must identify **workplace hazards** as part of context|
|Understand needs of customers & stakeholders|+ Explicitly includes **workers and their representatives** as key interested parties|
|QMS scope|OH&S scope must consider **worker activities, locations, and contractors**|

---

### Clause 5 – Leadership

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Top management commitment to quality|+ Stronger, more specific leadership accountability for **safety culture**|
|Customer focus|+ **Worker focus** — protecting physical and mental health|
|—|+ Requires leadership to **consult and actively involve workers** in OH&S decisions (sub-clause 5.4)|

> ISO 45001 goes further than ISO 9001 here — worker participation is a **dedicated sub-clause**, not just implied.

---

### Clause 6 – Planning _(Biggest Extension)_

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Risks and opportunities (quality-focused)|+ **Hazard identification** as a mandatory prerequisite step before risk assessment|
|Address risks to product/service conformity|+ OH&S risk assessment covering physical, chemical, ergonomic, and psychosocial hazards|
|—|+ **Hierarchy of Controls** (Eliminate → Substitute → Engineering → Administrative → PPE)|
|Legal compliance consideration|+ Stronger legal compliance obligation — proactive identification of OH&S laws and regulations|

---

### Clause 7 – Support

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Competence for quality tasks|+ Competence specifically for **safe work procedures**|
|Awareness of quality policy|+ Workers must be aware of **hazards, risks, and their right to remove themselves from danger**|
|Communication|+ Must address what, when, and who — with specific attention to worker safety communications|

---

### Clause 8 – Operation

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Operational planning and control|+ Control of hazards using the **hierarchy of controls**|
|Management of change|+ Change management must assess **OH&S impact** (new equipment, processes, chemicals)|
|Outsourcing / procurement|+ Must manage safety of **contractors, visitors, and outsourced processes**|
|—|+ **Emergency preparedness and response** (entirely new requirement not in ISO 9001)|

---

### Clause 9 – Performance Evaluation

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Monitoring and measurement|+ Monitor **leading indicators** (inspections, training) and **lagging indicators** (incidents, injuries)|
|Internal audit|Same concept, applied to OH&S|
|Management review|+ Must review **incident trends, hazard findings, and worker consultation outcomes**|

---

### Clause 10 – Improvement

|ISO 9001|ISO 45001 Extension|
|---|---|
|Nonconformity & corrective action|+ **Incident investigation** added — incidents trigger the same corrective action loop|
|Continual improvement|+ Improvement aimed at **eliminating hazards and reducing OH&S risk**|

---

## Summary: Core Extensions at a Glance

| Theme                 | ISO 9001                | ISO 45001 Adds                           |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Risk subject          | Product/process quality | Human safety and health                  |
| Hazard identification | Not present             | Mandatory precursor to risk assessment   |
| Controls              | Process controls        | Hierarchy of controls                    |
| People focus          | Customer satisfaction   | Worker protection & participation        |
| Incidents             | Nonconformities         | Incidents + near-misses + investigations |
| Emergency response    | Not required            | Required                                 |
| Worker rights         | Not addressed           | Right to refuse unsafe work              |

---

## Integration

Organizations commonly combine ISO 9001 + ISO 45001 (often with ISO 14001 for environment) into a single **Integrated Management System (IMS)**, reducing duplication of audits, documentation, and processes.

> **In essence:** ISO 45001 took the proven framework of ISO 9001 and layered on the specialized requirements needed when the "product" at risk is **human life and health** rather than goods or services.
